The Equation of Time

Why a sundial and a clock rarely agree, and by how much across the year.

The formula

Equation of time = apparent (sundial) solar time − mean (clock) solar time. A positive value means the sundial is ahead of the clock; negative means it's behind.

It comes from two combined effects: Earth's orbit is elliptical (it moves faster when nearer the Sun), and its axis is tilted relative to its orbit. A commonly used approximation, accurate to about a minute, is:

EoT (minutes) ≈ 9.87 sin(2B) − 7.53 cos(B) − 1.5 sin(B)
where B = (360°/365) × (d − 81), and d is the day of the year (1 to 365).

The graph and questions below use solarPosition.js's more precise calculation rather than this approximation, so don't be surprised if a hand calculation with the formula is off by a fraction of a minute.
