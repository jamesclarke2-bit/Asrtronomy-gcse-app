Earth's orbit

Not to scale. Earth's position comes from the sky diagram's declination (+23.44° at the June solstice, −23.44° at the December solstice, 0° at an equinox), so the two diagrams always agree. The red tick is Earth's rotation axis — notice it points the same screen direction at every position, whatever the season. That fixed tilt relative to the Sun, not distance, is what causes the seasons.
