Privacy

What this site is, what it stores on your own device, and how to clear it — in plain English, not legal text.

Last updated: 16 October 2026.

What this site is

GCSE Astronomy is a free set of interactive simulators and reference pages for the Pearson Edexcel GCSE Astronomy specification. Every page runs entirely in your own browser — there's no server behind it doing anything with what you do here.

No accounts, forms or cookies

There's nothing to sign up for, no account to create, and no form anywhere on the site that sends anything to anyone — including the number and text boxes on practice questions, which check your answer right here in your browser and are never submitted anywhere. This site sets no cookies at all.

What's stored on your device

The only thing this site keeps is one entry in your browser's own , named astro-quiz-progress. It holds how many practice questions you've got right and wrong so far, grouped by topic — numbers only, nothing that identifies you. It stays on this device and is never sent anywhere, to this site or anyone else's.

Clearing it

Use the button below, or clear it yourself through your browser's own settings — it's stored under this site's address, so clearing your browser's site data for this site removes it the same way.

Hosting and visitor logs

One thing is outside this site's own control: like any website, this one is hosted somewhere, and a host's own server logs typically see each visitor's IP address regardless of what the site itself does. This site is hosted on GitHub, which keeps its own logs under its own privacy policy, separate from anything described above.
